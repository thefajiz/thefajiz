project overview

what is swirath limousine?

a single-page luxury chauffeur website built to convert visitors into bookings via whatsapp, showcasing cross-border gcc coverage and a premium fleet.

context
a premium chauffeur, a digital presence to match
swirath limousine offers private cross-border transfers across the gcc. the brief was clear: build a website that looks and feels as premium as the service itself, with one goal: get the visitor to send a whatsapp message and book a ride.
approach
vibe-coded with lovable for rapid luxury delivery
built entirely using lovable's ai-powered platform, the site was designed with a deep navy starfield aesthetic, editorial serif typography, and a dead-simple booking flow. no forms, no friction, just one tap to whatsapp.
"
design philosophy
luxury is silence. one message. the gulf at your pace.
every copy line, layout choice, and interaction was built around a single idea: effortlessness. the site doesn't ask visitors to fill forms or wait for quotes. it shows the vehicle, the coverage, and a phone number. that's it.
screens

key screens

four sections, one seamless scroll. each screen was designed to build trust and reduce the distance between visitor and booking.

hero
animated starfield background, editorial headline "luxury chauffeur across the gulf", bahrain · saudi · qatar · uae tag, and three instant-contact buttons (whatsapp, instagram, email). five-star trust badge below the fold.
the fleet
gmc yukon xl flagship edition : 7 seats, leather, climate control, tinted windows. vehicle photo card with "yukon xl · black sapphire" badge and seat count. tap to reserve via whatsapp or instagram.
coverage
"four countries. one driver." country code cards for bh, sa, qa and ae with city detail. popular route bar: manama → dammam → riyadh → doha → dubai with a plan my route cta.
contact
"ready when you are." three contact cards: whatsapp primary (+973 3312 9577), email (infoswirathlimo@gmail.com), and instagram (@swirathlimo.bh). footer with social links and brand tagline.
features

what the site does

💬
instant whatsapp booking
the primary cta throughout the site links directly to whatsapp. one tap, no forms, no waiting. booking friction reduced to zero.
🗺️
gcc route coverage
cross-border service spanning bahrain, saudi arabia (dammam, riyadh, jeddah), qatar (doha) and uae (dubai, abu dhabi), clearly communicated with country code cards.
🚙
fleet showcase
dedicated fleet section featuring the gmc yukon xl black sapphire with full spec list and a high-quality vehicle photograph.
⭐
trust signals
five-star badge on the hero, "trusted by gcc travelers" copy, and an instagram presence card showing the fleet in action build credibility at a glance.
built with

tech stack

vibe-coded with lovable, an ai-powered app builder, for fast, high-quality output without writing boilerplate from scratch.

platform
lovable
ai-powered web app builder used to scaffold, design, and deploy the entire site through natural language prompting and iterative refinement.
frontend
react + tailwind
lovable outputs a clean react codebase with tailwind utility classes. the deep navy theme and starfield background were achieved through custom tailwind config and css layers.
deployment
lovable hosting
deployed instantly via lovable's built-in hosting at swirathlimo.lovable.app. no manual devops, no config, the site went live in minutes.
