project overview

what is v?

an end-to-end ai chatbot web application designed to provide accessible, stigma-free mental health support to anyone who needs it, particularly those facing financial, social, or logistical barriers to professional care.

context
a gap in mental health access, a digital companion
mental health challenges are widespread, yet professional therapy remains expensive, stigmatized, and logistically inaccessible for many. students, young professionals, and individuals in difficult home environments often have no viable alternative. v was designed to fill that gap with technology.
platform purpose
emotional support, wellness tracking, and coping tools in one place
users engage in ai-powered therapeutic conversations, complete daily mood assessments, follow personalized wellness plans, and access guided exercises. all interactions are stored securely and used to personalize future responses via the rag retrieval pipeline.
"
mission
making mental health support as accessible as sending a message
v acts as a 24/7 private companion that never judges, never charges per session, and never requires an appointment. it bridges the gap between self-help resources and professional care, particularly for users who are reluctant to seek help because of shame, cost, or a lack of nearby services.
problem and solution

why this platform was built

the majority of people experiencing anxiety, stress, and depression cannot access timely professional support due to cost, stigma, and logistics. existing digital tools lack the emotional intelligence to provide meaningful, context-aware responses.

platforms like woebot and wysa offer basic cbt-based interactions but rely on rigid, pre-programmed responses. none combine real-time generative ai with context retrieval, personalized wellness planning, and zero-knowledge data security.

  • professional therapy is unaffordable for most students and young adults
  • stigma and privacy concerns prevent many from seeking help
  • home environments often lack the privacy needed for therapy sessions
  • existing apps offer generic, non-personalized responses
  • long waiting times for professional appointments create critical gaps in care

the v solution

  • gemini-powered conversations, real-time, empathetic dialogue that understands emotional tone and adapts responses accordingly
  • rag retrieval pipeline, context from past conversations and curated mental health resources injected into every response
  • daily wellness plans, personalized task and exercise routines generated from the user's interaction history
  • mood assessment and tracking, onboarding check-ins feed the sentiment model and shape the chatbot's approach
  • zero-knowledge security, bcrypt hashing ensures not even developers can access user passwords
  • admin oversight layer, critical message flagging and system instruction management for responsible deployment
user interface

key screens and interactions

a fully responsive web application optimized for both desktop and mobile browsers, with consistent design language across all user-facing and admin screens.

homepage
landing page featuring the sign up / login flow, feature overview cards (ai chatbot, daily plans, personal insights, guided exercises), and primary call-to-action.

post-login onboarding check-in. users select their current emotional state, feeding the sentiment model that shapes v's initial tone and approach for the session.

virtual assistant

meet fatoom

v's built-in navigation assistant, designed to help users find features, ask questions, and feel guided throughout the platform without leaving the chat interface.

fatoom
virtual assistant · v platform guide

fatoom is the human-like virtual assistant embedded directly into the chat screen. she appears as a floating panel when users need help navigating the app, offering contextual suggestions and answers like "how can i assist you with the app today?" while users can ask about features, navigation, and available resources without interrupting their main conversation with v.

  • accessible from the chat screen at any time via a single tap
  • answers navigation questions without leaving the conversation
  • designed to reduce friction for first-time users
  • contributes to the platform's approachable, human-centred feel

user research

what the data said

a public survey of 30 participants informed v's feature set and design priorities. findings shaped everything from the mood assessment to the choice of coping tools offered.

83.3%
respondents aged 18 to 25
the primary demographic facing financial and logistical barriers to therapy. young adults and students formed the core target group for v's design.
53.3%
reported anxiety as a regular challenge
anxiety was the most commonly cited issue, followed by mood swings (50%), stress (46.7%), and lack of motivation (46.7%).
63.3%
face challenges several times a week
the high frequency of challenges confirmed the need for a 24/7 always-on platform rather than a scheduled or appointment-based system.
73.3%
rely on talking to friends or family
the most common coping mechanism was informal social support. v was designed to complement this by offering a non-judgmental, always-available alternative.
56.7%
open to an ai mental health chatbot
the majority expressed openness or interest. only 13.3% selected "yes" outright, reinforcing the need for a trustworthy, privacy-first design to convert the "maybe" group.
43.3%
most challenged at home or alone
home and personal alone time tied as the hardest environments, explaining why a private, accessible digital tool resonates more than in-person alternatives.
technology stack

how it was built

a modern full-stack architecture combining a react front-end, python api layer, google's gemini language model, and mongodb atlas for scalable, secure data management.

frontend
react.js
component-based ui with responsive layouts for desktop and mobile. manages chat history, wellness plans, exercises, stats, and admin views through a shared navigation system.
backend
python + fastapi
asynchronous api layer handling user authentication, message routing, rag retrieval, gemini api calls, and admin operations. integrates with sentence-transformers for embedding generation.
ai layer
google gemini api
state-of-the-art conversational language model providing empathetic, context-aware natural language responses. processes formatted conversation history and system instructions per request.
retrieval layer
rag + faiss
retrieval-augmented generation pipeline indexes curated mental health resources and chat history. faiss similarity search surfaces relevant context for every gemini response.
database
mongodb atlas
flexible document store housing user profiles, hashed credentials, chat logs, exercise templates, wellness checks, assessments, quiz responses, and critical message flags.
security
bcrypt + zero-knowledge
passwords hashed with bcrypt and salting before storage. zero-knowledge design means the original password is unrecoverable even by the development team, ensuring maximum user privacy.

what's next

planned enhancements

the modular architecture supports incremental expansion. new capabilities can be layered on without disrupting the existing codebase or user experience.

  • 01 · high priority
    mobile application
    a react native and expo app targeting both android and ios. a single shared codebase consuming all existing fastapi endpoints, enabling push notifications, offline mood logging, and native biometric authentication.
  • 02 · high priority
    advanced transformer models
    integration of emotion-specific transformer models fine-tuned on mental health datasets. this would deepen v's ability to detect subtle emotional nuances like masked anxiety or passive distress and tailor responses accordingly.
  • 03 · medium priority
    professional support bridge
    an in-app referral engine that identifies when a user's patterns suggest they need professional help, and connects them directly to certified therapists or counselors nearby or online, closing the gap between self-help and clinical care.
  • 04 · medium priority
    enhanced personalization engine
    deeper analysis of browsing history, preferred exercise types, and time-of-day engagement patterns to generate hyper-personalized wellness recommendations that evolve as the user's needs change over weeks and months.
